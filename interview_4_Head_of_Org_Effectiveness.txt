GNP FOUNDATION — INTERVIEW NOTES (4 of 5) | Head of Org Effectiveness

PAIN POINTS
- Mantra of CEO — how do we get integrated work done better?
- Leadership team is more aligned — one foundation, one voice
- Time to catch up with the world

STRATEGY
- Mission has evolved over the past few years

GRANTEE MESSAGES
- Processes are too convoluted
- Hard to build relationships with the Foundation
- Granting process takes too long
- Foundation isn't proactive or responsive
- Foundation doesn't understand the community or issue area in which I work
- Grant strategies aren't clear — not clear on what we're funding and why (not consistent between teams)
- We want grantees to see us as easy to work with, transparent, a partnering org, agile, etc.

PROGRAM OFFICERS
- National should be working with Geographic to develop a strategy and share what's working in other places
- Mission Driven Investments should be working with programs to align investments to missions

SILOS
- A lot fewer than years ago — breaking down barriers between the program and operations teams
- Grant Services — comes in too late with questions; due diligence around whether the budget matches the org plan, analysis of financial structure, meeting IRS requirements
- There are cliques — some by tenure, some by programs. 90% of our program officers have been here 5 years or less

DECISION MAKING
- CEO has created the environment to enable pushing down decisions and collaborating better as a leadership team
- We need to set out better criteria for what types of decisions need to get escalated to the CEO
- Empowered decision-making — people need to look beyond their direct responsibilities and just do the right thing. Decisional responsibility is not stated
- True accountability is a discipline leadership needs to build with each other and throughout the organization

STRENGTHS TO BE PRESERVED
- Passionate people — that passion becomes a part of our culture and how we get work done
- People like to be very engaged — that's a double-edged sword of inclusive decision making
- Supporting our community engagement model

ROADBLOCKS
- Shift in culture, breaking those silos — shedding light and transparency into people's roles
- People who are getting by doing little work, people who aren't aligned and can hide in the shadows
- Perceived notion that this might be CEO's flavor of the year
- People understanding and knowing how they fit in to this new way of working
- How we communicate this — this is so crucial
- Make sure the staff have the tools = social collaboration tools
