GNP FOUNDATION — INTERVIEW NOTES (3 of 5) | Head of Learning

PAIN POINTS
- Overly hierarchical
- Success — better positioned to execute on strategy
- Disrupted from creativity by hierarchy
- Increase in productivity, creative problem solve, self-sufficient

BARRIERS
- Decision Making (at the EC level)
- Peer Mentoring Program — couldn't get off the ground because too many cooks in the kitchen
- Training costs
- Facilities
- Hiring throughout the organization

COMMUNICATION
- People had a reorganized org for a year without announcing it or what the new roles are for contact points
- When someone is let go, the announcement is made but no supporting plan is provided

CHANGE MANAGEMENT / OTHER VENDOR RELATIONSHIPS
- Report directly to the CEO
- Other functions or projects are being blocked to prioritize this vendor
- Making decisions without evidence

REQUESTS FROM GRANTEES
- Speak plainly
- Wanting the Foundation to show a united face
- "How does the Foundation show up in these communities?"
- Cultivation process — who has the pulse of the community and the capacity to make the best impact?
- Grantees are increasingly asking for more strategy support, better understanding and community engagement

LACK OF ROLE CLARITY
- Program Manager, Officer, Specialist — what are the differences?
- Challenge of protecting silos — people knowledge is used as power; can make progress slow and difficult. Department = home; we need to change this

LIMITED SKILLS
- Writing
- Critical thinking
- Coaching — give and receive feedback
- Diplomacy / relationship management (internal and external)
- Productivity management (self-management and as member of a team)
- Emotional intelligence

PRESERVING STRENGTHS
- Smart people
- Mission driven
- Organizational DNA — values
- Work-life balance for those that have it
- "The focusing on one's own domain is well intentioned but not helpful to delivering effectively as an organization — the collaborative approach to the strategy papers demonstrated what can happen when people work across teams"

CORE FUNCTIONS
- How do we say no and sunset functions or grants that are no longer core — ask Head of Org Effectiveness

CHANGE MANAGEMENT
- A lot of people are relics and they are resistant to change — even the executives
- People are scarred by the 'lean down' back in 2009-10
- It's the change communication that really impairs effectiveness
